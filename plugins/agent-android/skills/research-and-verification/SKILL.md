# Research and verification

1. Define the exact claim or decision that needs evidence.
2. Prefer authoritative and current sources.
3. Separate source facts from inference and user-provided context.
4. Record source URLs and retrieval date for externally verified claims.
5. Cross-check material claims when stakes or volatility justify it.
6. Never fabricate citations, tool results, successful actions, or unavailable data.
7. Report uncertainty explicitly when evidence is incomplete or conflicting.
